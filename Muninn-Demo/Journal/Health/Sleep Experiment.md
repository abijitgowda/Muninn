# The Great Sleep Experiment

Tried to fix my sleep schedule. Here's what happened.

**Week 1 — No screens after 10pm**
Lasted 2 days. On day 3 I "just checked one Slack notification" at 10:15 and next thing I know it's midnight and I'm watching a documentary about octopus intelligence.

**Week 2 — Melatonin**
Worked great. Fell asleep at 10:30. Woke up at 3am wide awake. Stared at the ceiling for 2 hours thinking about that thing I said in a meeting in 2019.

**Week 3 — Blue light glasses**
Bought the $40 ones from Amazon. Pretty sure they're just clear lenses. Placebo effect lasted 3 days.

**Week 4 — Gave up and accepted 12:30am bedtime**
Productivity actually improved. Turns out I'm a night owl pretending to be a morning person because LinkedIn told me to.

Conclusion: the optimal sleep schedule is whatever schedule you'll actually follow.
