# App That Reminds You to Drink Water

I know there are 400 of these already. But hear me out.

This one connects to your calendar. Big meeting at 2pm? It reminds you to hydrate at 1:45 so you don't sound like a desert lizard on the call.

Features:
- Passive-aggressive notifications: "You've mass-produced 47 Slack messages without a sip. Your kidneys called."
- Integrates with Apple Health to track actual intake
- Gamification: compete with coworkers. Loser buys coffee.

Revenue model: freemium. Premium tier unlocks "Hydration Coach" which is just a recurring notification that says "drink water you absolute walnut."
