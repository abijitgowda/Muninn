# Things I Learned the Hard Way at Work

1. **Never deploy on Friday.** I know everyone says this. I didn't listen. Spent a Saturday rolling back a migration while my friends went hiking. The mountain will still be there Monday. So will the bug.

2. **"Quick fix" is never quick.** Estimated 30 minutes, took 3 days, broke 2 other features, and required a meeting to explain what happened.

3. **Write it down.** Six months from now, you won't remember why you made that weird architectural decision. Future-you will curse past-you. Leave a comment. Write an ADR. Something.

4. **Don't mass-reply-all.** Once cc'd the entire engineering org on a thread about a typo in the docs. 200 people now know I can't spell "authentication." Some of them still bring it up.

5. **Your manager doesn't read your PRs.** They read your Slack messages. Sad but true. Write good commit messages for yourself, write good Slack updates for your career.

6. **Take PTO.** Nobody on their deathbed wishes they'd closed one more Jira ticket.
