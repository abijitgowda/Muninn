## Must-do
- Tokyo: Akihabara (obvious), Tsukiji outer market, teamLab Borderless (if it's still open)

## Logistics
- JR Pass: 14-day costs ¥50,000. Math says it pays for itself if I take 3+ shinkansen rides.
- Pocket WiFi vs eSIM: eSIM is cheaper but last time I couldn't figure out APN settings for 45 minutes at Narita. Pocket WiFi. Pay the extra $20 for sanity.
- Cash is still king in small shops. Need to bring more than I think.
