# Should I Buy a House?

Ran the numbers. Then ran them again hoping they'd change. They didn't.

**Arguments for:**
- Building equity instead of paying someone else's mortgage
- Can finally get a dog
- No more landlord "inspections" where they judge my IKEA furniture

**Arguments against:**
- Down payment requires mass selling of my soul (or 3 more years of saving)
- Interest rates are doing whatever interest rates do when you're trying to buy
- Maintenance costs. My dad says "a house is just a money pit with a roof." He's been fixing his gutters every weekend since 1998.
- I'd have to commit to a city. I can barely commit to a lunch order.

**The math:**
- Current rent: $2,200/month
- Mortgage for equivalent house: $3,400/month (plus property tax, insurance, HOA)
- Difference: $1,200/month that could go into index funds
- But: in 30 years I'd own a house. In 30 years the index fund could also be worth a house. Or zero. Nobody knows.

Decision: revisit in 6 months. Same thing I said 6 months ago.
