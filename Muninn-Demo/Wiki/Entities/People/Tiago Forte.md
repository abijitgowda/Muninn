Tiago Forte is a productivity consultant, author, and educator best known for creating the Building a Second Brain (BASB) methodology — a systematic approach to [[Personal Knowledge Management]] that has attracted hundreds of thousands of students through his online courses and his 2022 book of the same name. Forte's framework centers on the PARA organizational system (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives) and the CODE workflow (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express), providing a practical structure that works across any digital note-taking tool. His influence has been particularly strong in the [[Obsidian]] and Notion communities, where BASB principles shape how users structure their vaults and workspaces.

Forte's work builds on earlier [[Personal Knowledge Management]] traditions, particularly the [[Zettelkasten]] method, while adding an emphasis on actionability and output. Where Zettelkasten focuses on building a network of atomic ideas for intellectual development, the [[Second Brain]] methodology is explicitly oriented toward producing creative work — writing, presentations, projects. Forte's technique of "progressive summarization" (layering bold, highlight, and summary on captured material) is designed to reduce future retrieval friction, ensuring that the most important ideas are immediately visible without re-reading entire notes. His integration of [[Spaced Repetition]] principles into review workflows further bridges the gap between storage and active recall.

The rise of AI-powered knowledge management tools has created both challenges and opportunities for Forte's methodology. The [[LLM Wiki]] pattern automates many of the steps that BASB requires humans to perform manually — capture, distillation, linking, and organization. Forte has acknowledged this shift, noting that AI will likely handle the "bookkeeping" of knowledge management while humans focus on the creative and strategic decisions about what to capture and how to apply it. This evolution suggests that the future of the Second Brain may be less about human discipline and more about human curation of AI-maintained knowledge systems.
