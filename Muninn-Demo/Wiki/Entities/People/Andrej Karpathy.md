Andrej Karpathy is a Slovak-Canadian computer scientist who has shaped the trajectory of modern deep learning through both research and education. He was a founding member of [[OpenAI]] in 2015 and later served as Senior Director of AI at Tesla from 2017 to 2022, where he led the Autopilot computer vision team responsible for the neural-network-based approach to self-driving. He returned briefly to OpenAI in 2023 before departing to focus on AI education and independent projects.

Karpathy is widely recognized for his ability to make complex AI concepts accessible. His Stanford CS231n lectures on convolutional neural networks became one of the most popular deep learning courses ever offered, and his YouTube tutorials on building neural networks from scratch have millions of views. His educational philosophy emphasizes understanding through implementation — building things from the ground up rather than relying on high-level abstractions.

In early 2025, Karpathy published a GitHub Gist outlining the [[LLM Wiki]] concept: a persistent, interlinked knowledge base that an LLM maintains over time as an alternative to stateless [[Retrieval Augmented Generation]]. The idea resonated widely in the developer community and inspired multiple open-source implementations, including projects that use [[Obsidian]] as the viewing layer and Claude Code or similar agents as the maintenance engine.
