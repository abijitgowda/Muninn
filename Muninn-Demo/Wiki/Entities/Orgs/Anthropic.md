Anthropic is an [[AI Safety]] company founded in 2021 by [[Dario Amodei]] and Daniela Amodei, along with several other former [[OpenAI]] researchers. The company's mission centers on building AI systems that are interpretable, steerable, and robust — what they describe as "AI safety as a science." Anthropic is headquartered in San Francisco and has raised over $7 billion in funding from investors including [[Google]], Spark Capital, and Salesforce Ventures, making it one of the most well-capitalized AI startups in history.

Anthropic's primary product is Claude, a family of large language models that compete directly with OpenAI's GPT series and Google's Gemini. Claude is distinguished by its emphasis on helpfulness, harmlessness, and honesty — principles derived from Anthropic's research on constitutional AI, a technique where the model is trained to follow a set of principles rather than relying solely on human feedback. The Claude model family spans multiple capability tiers: Haiku for fast, lightweight tasks; Sonnet for balanced performance; and Opus for the most complex reasoning. Claude Code, a CLI-based coding agent, has become a particularly influential tool in the developer ecosystem.

The company's approach to safety has influenced the broader AI landscape, including how organizations think about [[LLM Wiki]]-style knowledge systems that require models to maintain persistent, source-tracked knowledge bases. [[Geoffrey Hinton]], who has been vocal about AI existential risk, has cited Anthropic's safety-first approach as a model for the industry, though he has also cautioned that no single company's efforts can solve alignment in isolation.
