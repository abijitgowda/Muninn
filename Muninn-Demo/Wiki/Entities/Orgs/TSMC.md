Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) is the world's largest dedicated semiconductor foundry, manufacturing chips designed by other companies rather than selling its own branded processors. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang in Hsinchu, Taiwan, TSMC pioneered the pure-play foundry model that separated chip design from fabrication and transformed the semiconductor industry. The company fabricates processors for virtually every major technology company: [[NVDA]] ([[NVIDIA]])'s AI GPUs, [[AMD]]'s Ryzen and EPYC processors, Apple's M-series chips, and Qualcomm's mobile SoCs are all manufactured on TSMC's process nodes. This concentration makes TSMC arguably the most strategically important company in the global technology supply chain.

TSMC's dominance rests on its ability to consistently lead in advanced process technology. The company's 3nm and 2nm nodes (measured by transistor density rather than actual feature size) enable the chip designs that power AI infrastructure. [[Jensen Huang]]'s NVIDIA and [[Lisa Su]]'s AMD both depend entirely on TSMC for manufacturing their latest GPU architectures — the Blackwell and MI series respectively. [[Google]]'s TPU chips and Amazon's Graviton/Trainium processors are also TSMC products. This means that the entire AI hardware ecosystem, from the GPUs training [[Transformer Architecture]] models at [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] to the inference chips serving [[Google DeepMind]]'s Gemini, flows through TSMC's fabrication facilities.

The geopolitical dimension of TSMC's position has become a major factor in global technology strategy. Taiwan's proximity to mainland China and the concentration of advanced semiconductor manufacturing on the island have prompted the United States and Europe to invest billions in domestic chip fabrication through the CHIPS Act and similar programs. TSMC is building fabs in Arizona and Japan, but replicating its Taiwan-based ecosystem — where decades of supply chain integration, engineering talent, and process expertise converge — is a multi-year undertaking. The company's [[TSM]] stock is closely watched as a barometer of both AI investment trends and geopolitical risk.
