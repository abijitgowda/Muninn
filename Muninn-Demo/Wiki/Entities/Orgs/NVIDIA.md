# NVIDIA

[[NVIDIA]] is a leading technology company renowned for its graphics processing units (GPUs) and AI infrastructure solutions. The Nemotron 3 Nano Omni and Nemotron 3 Super models from NVIDIA represent cutting-edge multimodal large language models, with the latter being an open [[Mixture of Experts (MoE)]] model that activates only 12 billion parameters to optimize compute efficiency for intricate multi-agent applications.
