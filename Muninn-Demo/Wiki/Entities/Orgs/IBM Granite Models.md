# IBM Granite Models

[[IBM Granite Models]] are a suite of [[Open Foundation Models]] developed by IBM, designed for enterprise use with capabilities in multilingual processing, coding, [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)]], tool utilization, and structured JSON output. Released under the permissive Apache 2.0 license, they aim to provide robust, scalable AI solutions for businesses while maintaining transparency and accessibility.
