Alphabet Inc. (NASDAQ: GOOG/GOOGL) is the parent company of [[Google]] and [[Google DeepMind]], and one of the largest technology companies in the world by market capitalization. Founded as Google in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin, the company restructured under the Alphabet holding company in 2015. Alphabet generates the vast majority of its revenue from Google's advertising business, but its strategic investments in AI, cloud computing, and autonomous vehicles (Waymo) position it as a diversified technology conglomerate. Google Cloud, the company's cloud infrastructure division, competes with AWS and Azure and has become a key distribution channel for [[Google DeepMind]]'s AI models.

Alphabet's AI investments span both research and infrastructure. The company designs its own Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), custom AI accelerators that compete with [[NVDA]]'s GPUs for training and inference workloads. TPUs power Google's internal AI infrastructure and are available to external customers through Google Cloud, giving Alphabet a vertically integrated position in the AI hardware stack that [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] (both dependent on [[NVIDIA]] GPUs) cannot match. The Gemini model family, developed by Google DeepMind, is integrated across Google's product portfolio including Search, Workspace, and Android.

The stock has historically traded at a discount to pure-play AI companies due to investor concerns about AI disrupting Google's core search advertising business. However, Alphabet's AI-generated revenue has grown rapidly through cloud AI services and Gemini API access. The company's $2 billion investment in Anthropic (as a cloud partner, distinct from Amazon's larger investment) illustrates its strategy of maintaining relationships across the AI ecosystem while developing its own competitive models through [[Google DeepMind]].
