# Mistral AI

[[Mistral AI]] is a pioneering artificial intelligence research laboratory known for its innovative large language models. The Mistral Medium 3.5 model, introduced as the first flagship from Mistral AI, integrates instruction-following, reasoning, and coding within a unified architecture of 128 billion parameters, marking a significant advancement in multimodal and agentic AI capabilities.
