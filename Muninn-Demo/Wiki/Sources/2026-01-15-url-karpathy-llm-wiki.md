[[Andrej Karpathy]] published a GitHub Gist outlining the concept of an [[LLM Wiki]] — a persistent, interlinked knowledge base maintained by large language models. The central argument is that pure [[Retrieval Augmented Generation]] is wasteful because it re-derives answers from scratch every query, never accumulating a coherent knowledge layer. The wiki alternative compiles knowledge once into markdown files, tracks provenance, and uses [[Spreading Activation]] for brain-like retrieval across `[[wikilinks]]`. The pattern has implications for [[Personal Knowledge Management]], shifting the organizational burden from humans to LLMs.
