A comprehensive explainer article covering the [[Transformer Architecture]] from its origins in the 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper through its current dominance in AI systems. The article traces the technical foundations of the [[Attention Mechanism]], discusses how [[Context Window]] sizes have expanded from 2K to over 1M tokens, and covers practical applications from [[Fine-tuning]] to [[Agent Framework]] architectures. The competitive landscape section profiles key organizations including [[OpenAI]], [[Google DeepMind]], and their reliance on [[GPU Computing]] hardware from companies like [[NVDA]] (manufactured by [[TSMC]]). The article also addresses [[AI Safety]] considerations and the [[Open Source AI]] movement's impact on the accessibility of transformer-based models.
