# Mixture of Experts (MoE)

A Mixture of Experts architecture in AI involves routing inputs through a selection of specialized neural network modules based on their expertise. This method, used by models like [[NVIDIA]] Nemotron 3 Super, optimizes computational efficiency by activating only the most relevant experts for each query, thereby reducing resource consumption without sacrificing performance.
