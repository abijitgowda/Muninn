# Agentic Workflows

Agentic workflows in AI refer to systems where autonomous agents perform sequential tasks based on predefined objectives and reasoning capabilities. These workflows are crucial for complex problem-solving scenarios, enabling models like MiniMax-M2.5 and Z.ai's large model to execute long-horizon tasks with minimal human intervention.
