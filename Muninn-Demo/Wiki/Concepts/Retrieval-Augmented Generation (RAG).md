# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Retrieval-Augmented Generation is an AI technique that enhances large language models by integrating external information retrieval processes during text generation. This approach allows models to access and incorporate up-to-date, domain-specific data, improving the accuracy and relevance of generated content while mitigating issues like hallucination.
