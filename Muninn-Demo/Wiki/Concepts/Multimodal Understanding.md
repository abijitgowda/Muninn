# Multimodal Understanding

Multimodal understanding in AI involves processing and integrating information from multiple sensory modalities such as text, images, audio, and video. Models like [[NVIDIA]] Nemotron 3 Nano Omni are designed to unify these modalities for enterprise applications, facilitating tasks ranging from Q&A and summarization to document intelligence.
