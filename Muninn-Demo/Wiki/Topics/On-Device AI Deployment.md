# On-Device AI Deployment

On-device AI deployment refers to running machine learning models directly on end-user devices like smartphones or laptops, minimizing latency and data privacy concerns by avoiding cloud-based inference. Gemma4's edge-focused variants (E2B and E4B) are specifically engineered for this purpose, offering compact model sizes and optimized compute paths suitable for local execution without compromising essential functionalities such as reasoning and coding assistance.
