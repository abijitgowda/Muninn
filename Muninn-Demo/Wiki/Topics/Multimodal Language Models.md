# Multimodal Language Models

Multimodal language models integrate text processing with other modalities like images and audio, allowing them to understand and generate responses based on visual or auditory inputs. Gemma4 exemplifies this trend by supporting variable aspect ratios and resolutions for image input, enhancing its utility in applications requiring cross-modal understanding, such as visual question answering and multimodal reasoning tasks.
