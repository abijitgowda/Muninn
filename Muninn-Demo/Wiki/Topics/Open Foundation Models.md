# Open Foundation Models

Open foundation models are large-scale AI models released under open licenses, allowing unrestricted access and modification. This trend is evident in the [[IBM Granite Models]] and Gemma series, promoting transparency, collaboration, and innovation across industries while addressing ethical considerations related to model deployment.
