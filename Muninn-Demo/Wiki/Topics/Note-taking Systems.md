Note-taking systems encompass the tools, methodologies, and philosophies that individuals and organizations use to capture and structure information. The history spans from commonplace books of the Renaissance, through Niklas Luhmann's [[Zettelkasten]] in the 20th century, to the explosion of digital tools in the 2010s and 2020s. Modern note-taking has been shaped by two competing paradigms: hierarchical organization (folders, notebooks, tags) exemplified by Evernote and Notion, and networked organization (bidirectional links, graph views) exemplified by [[Obsidian]], Roam Research, and Logseq. The networked paradigm draws directly from the Zettelkasten tradition and has gained significant momentum since 2020.

The current landscape is defined by several major frameworks. [[Tiago Forte]]'s [[Second Brain]] methodology emphasizes capture and progressive summarization within a PARA structure. The Zettelkasten method prioritizes atomic notes and emergent structure through dense interlinking. Cornell notes, mind mapping, and outline-based systems each serve different cognitive styles and use cases. [[Spaced Repetition]] intersects with note-taking through tools like Anki that transform notes into active recall exercises. What unites these approaches is the recognition that the value of notes lies not in the act of writing them but in the system's ability to surface relevant information when needed — a principle that connects directly to information retrieval concepts like [[Retrieval Augmented Generation]] and [[Spreading Activation]].

The emergence of AI-augmented note-taking represents a paradigm shift in the field. The [[LLM Wiki]] concept removes the human bottleneck from note maintenance: rather than requiring the user to manually link, tag, and organize, a large language model handles these tasks automatically. Systems like [[Muninn]] ingest raw sources and produce interlinked wiki pages without human intervention, essentially automating the organizational discipline that traditional note-taking systems demand but most users cannot sustain.
